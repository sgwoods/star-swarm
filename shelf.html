The shelf

Variations of Star Swarm's games that other players made, chosen to keep. Each one is the same game with something changed — the ships it starts with, its sound, its packs or its stages — and says who made it.

Play opens the game with that variation chosen: press Enter to take it, and Enter again to play. On this page Tab reaches each Play and Enter takes it; Esc goes back to the game.

Made one you like? Put it on the shelf: export it from the game and open a pull request adding that one file.

Every variation here was flown by the game's own playability check before it was published: it loads, every stage builds and stays on screen, and the game's autoplay personas finish it. The reference games are never changed by one.
